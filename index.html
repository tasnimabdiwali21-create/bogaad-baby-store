Our story

Little ones deserve a little extra love.

Bogaad Baby Store is a baby and children's essentials store based in Mogadishu, Somalia. We aim to make it easier for parents to explore baby care products, clothing, toys and everyday essentials in one welcoming place.

We believe shopping for your little one should feel simple, helpful and joyful. Our team is happy to answer your questions and help you check product availability.

  • A thoughtful range of baby and children's essentials
  • Friendly service and direct WhatsApp support
  • Clear product information before you order
